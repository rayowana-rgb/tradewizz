Delete Your TradeWizz Account

Last Updated: June 2026

TradeWizz allows users to create accounts for simulated portfolio tracking.

Users may request permanent deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

Contact Support for Account Deletion

How to Request Deletion

Option 1 — In the app

  • Open the TradeWizz app
  • Navigate to Account Settings
  • Select Delete Account (if available)

Option 2 — By email

Send an email to: rayowana@gmail.com

Subject: Delete TradeWizz Account

Include:

  • Registered email address
  • Request for account deletion

Data Deleted

When an account deletion request is processed, the following data will be removed:

  • User profile information
  • Simulated portfolio data
  • Watchlists
  • Portfolio performance history
  • User preferences linked to the account

Data Retention

TradeWizz may retain limited information for:

  • Security purposes
  • Fraud prevention
  • Legal compliance

Any retained data will be deleted after the legally required retention period.

Processing Timeline

30
Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days.
