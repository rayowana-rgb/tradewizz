TradeWizz Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Introduction

TradeWizz is an educational stock analysis and portfolio simulation application designed to help users analyze stocks, track watchlists, and manage simulated portfolios.

Information We Collect

  • Watchlists you create
  • Portfolio simulation data
  • Trade journal entries
  • App preferences and settings
  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Anonymous diagnostic information
  • Crash reports

Information We Do Not Collect

  • Bank account information
  • Credit card information
  • Brokerage account credentials
  • Government-issued identification
  • Real-money trading instructions

How We Use Information

  • Provide application features
  • Save user preferences
  • Improve app performance
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Enhance future product features

Data Sharing

TradeWizz does not sell personal information.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information.

Third-Party Services

TradeWizz may use third-party providers for market data, cloud infrastructure, analytics, and crash reporting.

Children's Privacy

TradeWizz is not intended for children under 13 years old.

Financial Disclaimer

TradeWizz is an educational and research tool.

TradeWizz does not provide financial advice, investment recommendations, brokerage services, or real-money trading services.

Users are solely responsible for their investment decisions.
